# Contributing

PolGuard is built and maintained by the Web3 Foundation SecOps team for our own monitoring needs.
We've open sourced it because it may be useful to others, not because we're looking for
contributors.

**What that means in practice:**

- Issues and pull requests may not get a response, and may be closed without discussion.
- We won't add features we don't need ourselves.
- There's no roadmap, no release cadence, and no support commitment.
- **Forking is encouraged.**

If you do open a PR, keep it small and make sure `yarn lint`, `yarn test` and
`yarn test:integration` pass. By contributing you agree your work is licensed under
[Apache-2.0](LICENSE), per section 5 of that license.

Security issues are the exception — please **don't** open a public issue. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
